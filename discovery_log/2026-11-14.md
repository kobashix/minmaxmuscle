### 2. Cyclized Hexapeptide-9 (CHP-9)

**Category:** Skin & Hair Health
**Legal Status:** Cosmetic Grade
**Slug:** `cyclized-hexapeptide-9`
**Mechanism:** Integrin β4 agonist at dermal-epidermal junction; stimulates laminin-332 and type IV collagen; enhanced protease stability and permeability via N-to-C cyclization; 6× integrin β4 activity vs linear hexapeptide-9
**Key Evidence:**
- Randomized, double-blinded, active- (retinol) and vehicle-controlled clinical trial (PMC12207714, PMID 40586182, *Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology*, Wiley DOI 10.1111/jocd.70290, indexed June/July 2025)
- CHP-9 reduced crow's feet and forehead wrinkles (number, area, roughness) at Day 56
- Outperformed retinol on all metrics except crow's feet roughness (statistically significantly larger effect sizes)
- Skin hydration significantly higher vs retinol at Day 56
- Progressive time course: effects start smaller than retinol but build to exceed retinol by Day 56

**Rank:** 92

## Summary

**2 new qualifying peptides discovered and staged: ABBV-295 and Cyclized Hexapeptide-9 (CHP-9).**

**ABBV-295** (AbbVie/Gubra) is the third major-pharma amylin analog to enter human development alongside cagrilintide (Novo Nordisk) and eloralintide (Lilly). Its distinguishing pharmacokinetic feature is a ~11–12 day half-life — the longest in the class — enabling investigation of monthly dosing in Phase 2. With Phase 2 (NCT07752979, N=360) initiated August 2026 and Phase 1 showing ~9.8% weight loss at 12 weeks, it is the most recent and least-advanced of the three amylin programs but represents a potential convenience differentiator if monthly efficacy is confirmed.

**Cyclized Hexapeptide-9 (CHP-9)** was previously rejected for insufficient evidence. This run identified a 2025 peer-reviewed RCT (PMC12207714, *Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology*) showing CHP-9 outperforms retinol on wrinkle number, area, roughness, and skin hydration in a double-blinded controlled trial. As the first cosmetic peptide staged from the skin category, it fills a gap in the site's coverage and is relevant to users seeking non-retinol skin aging solutions.

**ObesityWeek 2026 (November 14–17) is the active monitoring window for the next several runs.** The ASC37 poster tonight (preclinical) and DA-1726 comparison poster (tonight) are the immediate priority. ZUPREME-1 posthoc and ZYNERGY-related presentations Sunday (Nov 16–17) are higher-stakes events for site entries.
