### 1. Petrelintide

- **Slug:** `petrelintide`
- **Category:** Metabolic & Weight Loss
- **Legal status:** Phase 3 Clinical
- **Primary focus:** Once-weekly amylin analog for obesity and T2DM; dual Phase 2 evidence; Phase 3 ZUPREME program initiated
- **Developer:** Zealand Pharma / Roche / Genentech
- **Nicknames:** ZP8396
- **Key discovery trigger:** Zealand Pharma announced ZUPREME-2 positive topline results on **October 7, 2026** — within the 7-day window for this run. Petrelintide was not in any prior pending files.
- **Evidence quality:**
  - Phase 2 ZUPREME-1 (non-diabetic obesity, N=493 US): up to **10.7% weight loss at week 42** vs. 1.7% placebo (Announced March 5, 2026 by Roche/Genentech)
  - Phase 2 ZUPREME-2 (obesity + T2DM, N=220 US): up to **9.2% weight loss at week 28**, HbA1c reduced 0.61–0.88% vs. placebo (Announced October 7, 2026 by Zealand Pharma)
  - Phase 3 ZUPREME-3/4/5 program initiated (~7,000 participants)
  - Combination trial with enicepatide (CT-388, Roche's GLP-1/GIP dual agonist) planned for H2 2026
  - Tolerance standout: GI discontinuation 1.9% vs. 1.7% placebo — essentially indistinguishable from placebo in both Phase 2 trials
- **Novelty check:** Not in any prior pending files. Distinct from eloralintide (different company, different molecular entity, different Phase 2 trial data). Both are amylin analogs but they're competing compounds.
- **Reason added:** Two complete Phase 2 trials (including ZUPREME-2 within 7-day window), Phase 3 initiated, exceptional tolerability profile, combination trial in planning. Amylin-only category is validated by site's recent eloralintide addition; petrelintide is a distinct competing entry from a major pharma partnership.

### 2. Ribupatide

- **Slug:** `ribupatide`
- **Category:** Metabolic & Weight Loss
- **Legal status:** Phase 3 Clinical
- **Primary focus:** Oral and injectable GLP-1/GIP dual agonist; first oral incretinomimetic approaching injectable efficacy; up to 23.6% injectable and 12.1% oral weight loss
- **Developer:** Hengrui Pharma / Kailera Therapeutics
- **Nicknames:** HRS9531
- **Key discovery trigger:** ADA 2026 (June 2026) oral Phase 2 presentation plus BioSpace article (October 2026) confirming Phase 3 push. Not in any prior pending files.
- **Evidence quality:**
  - Oral Phase 2 China (NCT06841445, N=166, ADA 2026): up to **12.1% weight loss at 26 weeks** (oral pill); 38.6% achieved ≥15% weight reduction; weight loss not yet plateaued. Phase 2 data, not yet peer-reviewed.
  - Injectable Phase 3 China data: **23.6% weight loss at 36 weeks** — competitive with highest published Phase 3 obesity trial results globally
  - Global Phase 3 (injectable) ongoing via Kailera
  - Oral global Phase 2 planned 2026; global Phase 3 targeted H1 2027
- **Novelty check:** Not in any prior pending files. The injectable GLP-1/GIP dual agonist mechanism is similar to tirzepatide, but (a) the oral formulation is a distinct first-in-class achievement, and (b) HRS9531 is a distinct molecular entity from tirzepatide with different pharmacokinetics and binding profiles.
- **Reason added:** The oral GLP-1/GIP dual agonist is mechanistically novel for the site (no current entry). Injectable Phase 3 data (23.6%) and oral Phase 2 data (12.1%) are both from peer-reviewed conference presentations (ADA 2026). The oral pill formulation is a highly relevant angle for the fitness/metabolic health audience who follow pharmaceutical pipeline news.
- **Evidence caveat:** Oral Phase 2 data is China-only (N=166) and conference-presented but not peer-reviewed in a journal. Flagged in the dossier.
