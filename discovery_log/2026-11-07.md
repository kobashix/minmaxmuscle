- **Category:** Metabolic & Weight Loss
- **Legal status:** FDA Approved
- **Rank:** 94
- **Primary focus:** 8-amino acid macrocyclic peptide; MC4R selective agonist; reactivates the broken leptin-melanocortin pathway in patients with POMC/PCSK1/LEPR deficiency, Bardet-Biedl syndrome, or acquired hypothalamic obesity
- **Developer:** Rhythm Pharmaceuticals
- **Nicknames:** Imcivree, RM-493
- **Key discovery trigger:**

  - **March 2026 AHO FDA approval** (not previously captured by pipeline): FDA approved setmelanotide for acquired hypothalamic obesity (March 2026) — the first FDA-approved therapy for this condition. This approval was missed by prior discovery runs (which focused on GLP-1/metabolic compounds).
- **Evidence quality:**
  - Phase 3 POMC deficiency (Clément et al., NEJM 2020): 25.6% weight reduction in POMC cohort, 12.5% in LEPR cohort, vs. <0.5% placebo (p<0.001)

  - FDA approved for 3 indications; approved for pediatric patients ≥6 years for POMC/LEPR/BBS indications

The MC4R pathway story is genuinely educational — it explains what happens when the body's own satiety signal is genetically absent, and how a peptide drug can restore it. First MC4R-pathway compound in the discovery pipeline.

The pineal gland → telomerase → aging story is a genuinely unique scientific concept with peer-reviewed (if single-group) evidence.
