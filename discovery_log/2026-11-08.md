  - TRIUMPH-1 (Phase 3, obesity): up to 28.3% weight loss at 80 weeks (12 mg); 45.3% of participants lost ≥30% body weight

  - TRIUMPH-3 (Phase 3, severe obesity + CVD): 22.6% weight loss; MACE-5 HR 0.82 (CI crossed 1.0)

No other compound in the discovery pipeline includes a glucagon receptor agonist component. The 28.3% weight loss number is unprecedented in clinical trial history for any oral or injectable obesity drug. The site currently has semaglutide-class entries (amycretin/eloralintide as GLP-1 relatives) and GLP-1/glucagon duals (pemvidutide, mazdutide, survodutide), but no GLP-1/GIP/glucagon triple agonist. Retatrutide is the clinical culmination of this pharmacological strategy.
