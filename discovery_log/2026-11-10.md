| **Pemvidutide** (already staged Oct 29) | RECLAIM Phase 2 AUD results, AASLD Nov 9, 2026 | Significant new indication data; update existing D1 entry once accessible |

**Pemvidutide RECLAIM AUD Detail (AASLD November 9, 2026):**
- **Primary endpoint:** Statistically significant reduction in heavy drinking days (HDD) per week vs placebo — primary endpoint met
- **Key finding:** 42.2% of pemvidutide-treated patients had no heavy drinking days during the last 4 weeks, vs 17.4% on placebo
- **Biomarker:** 38% reduction in PEth (phosphatidylethanol, a direct alcohol biomarker) vs +5.9% increase on placebo — the "strongest validation for pemvidutide in this indication" per multiple analyses
- **Safety:** Well-tolerated; discontinuation 20% drug vs 22% placebo (comparable, slightly *lower* than placebo)
- **Next steps:** FDA End-of-Phase 2 meeting scheduled for December 2026 to discuss Phase 3 program design
