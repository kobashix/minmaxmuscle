### 2. MOTS-c

- **Slug:** `mots-c`
- **Category:** Immune & Longevity
- **Legal status:** Research Only
- **Rank:** 93
- **Primary focus:** 16-amino acid mitochondria-derived peptide (MDP) encoded in mitochondrial 12S rRNA; activates AMPK independent of AMP/ATP ratio; exercise mimetic; natural levels rise with exercise, decline with aging and T2D; FDA PCAC favorable vote 7-2 (July 2026); HHS Interim Enforcement Discretion Policy (October 28, 2026)
- **Developer:** Discovery: Pinchas Cohen lab, USC (2015). No commercial developer; compound in research phase with compounding pharmacy access now opened by HHS policy.
- **Nicknames:** Mitochondrial Open Reading Frame of the 12S rRNA-c, MOTS-C, MDP
- **Key discovery trigger:**
  - **HHS Interim Enforcement Discretion Policy (~October 28, 2026):** HHS issued a policy directing FDA not to take enforcement action against state-licensed compounding pharmacies preparing MOTS-c (and 5 other peptides) under specific quality conditions. This is directly within the 7-day window (October 28 – November 6, 2026).
  - **November 5, 2026 GlobalNewswire release:** "Federal Peptide Policy Shift Could Ignite a New Wave Of Growth Across the $164 Billion Peptide Market" — directly discusses the HHS policy impact on MOTS-c and related peptides.
  - **FDA PCAC 7-2 favorable vote:** July 23-24, 2026 (FDA's advisory committee supported 503A Bulks List inclusion).
- **Evidence quality:**
  - Foundational preclinical: Lee et al., Cell Metabolism, 2015 — HFD mice, improved insulin sensitivity, reduced adiposity (high-quality peer-reviewed journal).
  - Exercise mimetic: Kim et al., Cell Metabolism, 2018 — Aging mice: improved running capacity and grip strength without formal exercise (peer-reviewed).
  - Lifespan extension: Nature Communications — 11% median lifespan extension in 23-month-old male mice (late-life intervention).
  - Human observational: Reynolds et al., Nature Communications, 2021 — 12-fold muscle MOTS-c rise post-exercise; lower levels in older adults and T2D vs. controls (n=10, observational, peer-reviewed).
  - Human interventional: MOTS-MET Phase 2a (NCT07505745) recruiting — 120 adults with prediabetes, 12-week placebo-controlled. Results expected 2027.
  - **Caveat:** No completed human efficacy RCT. FDA staff opposed the PCAC recommendation. The favorable policy reflects advisory recommendation and HHS/FDA administration priorities more than clinical evidence of human efficacy.
- **Novelty check:** Not in any prior pending files. No MDP (mitochondria-derived peptide) class entry currently on the site. MOTS-c is mechanistically distinct from all compounds in the pipeline — the first peptide in the pipeline to be encoded in mitochondrial DNA.
- **Reason added:** HHS enforcement discretion policy (Oct 28, 2026) is a direct 7-day trigger. Strong peer-reviewed preclinical evidence meets the "significant preclinical data in peer-reviewed journals" criterion. The concept that mitochondria produce peptide hormones that signal metabolic state — including exercise-responsive, age-declining hormones — is exceptionally educational for a performance and longevity audience. First peptide in the discovery pipeline from the MDP class.
