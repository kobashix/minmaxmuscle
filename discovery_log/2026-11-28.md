## New Peptides Added — November 28, 2026

### 1. Elamipretide (SS-31)

- **Developer:** Stealth BioTherapeutics (commercial: Forzinity)
- **Category:** Repair & Recovery
- **Slug:** `elamipretide`
- **Rank:** 75
- **Status:** FDA Approved (Barth syndrome, accelerated approval Sept 19, 2025)
- **Mechanism:** Cardiolipin binding in inner mitochondrial membrane → cristae stabilization → ETC supercomplex maintenance → improved ATP efficiency, reduced ROS
- **Key differentiator:** Only FDA-approved mitochondria-targeting peptide; works without intact membrane potential; structural (not biochemical) mechanism vs. CoQ10/NAD+ approaches
- **Evidence base:**
  - TAZPOWER Phase 2/3 trial (Barth syndrome, n=12, significant knee extensor improvement)
  - 168-week open-label extension (sustained benefit)
  - Ex vivo failing human heart data (significant mitochondrial respiration improvement)
  - 2026 Circ Heart Fail (HFpEF rat model, skeletal muscle improvement)
  - Aged mouse longevity data (preserved function, no epigenetic age change)
