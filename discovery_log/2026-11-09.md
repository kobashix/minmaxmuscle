The 68% liver fat reduction at Phase 1 interim is a genuinely striking early-stage number for a MASLD/MASH-targeted compound. The ObesityWeek 2026 comparison poster to retatrutide adds timely relevance. The oxyntomodulin scaffold story (solving the half-life problem of native OXM, which showed efficacy 15 years ago) adds scientific depth. MASLD/MASH is an underserved category on the site.
