## New Peptides Added — November 17, 2026

### PT-00114
- **Category:** Cognitive & Neuroprotective
- **Status:** Phase 1 Clinical (ex-US completed; US Phase 1/2a planned Q2 2027)
- **Rank:** 63
- **Developer:** Protagenic Therapeutics (NASDAQ: PTIX), Toronto, Canada / New York
- **Mechanism:** 41-aa synthetic analog of TCAP-1 neuropeptide; CRH inhibitor; HPA axis modulation via CRF pathway; latrophilin (ADGRL/CIRL) GPCR; reduces cortisol/corticosterone upstream; subcutaneous administration
- **Indications:** Generalized Anxiety Disorder (Phase 1/2a planned), PTSD (Phase 2 track described), Major Depressive Disorder (exploratory)
- **7-Day Trigger:** November 11, 2026 — private placement announcement ($5M)
- **Supporting Evidence:**
  - Al Chawaf et al. 2007 (Behavioural Brain Research): CRF-induced anxiety suppressed by TCAP-1 (open field + EPM)
  - Tan et al. 2008 (Psychoneuroendocrinology): Acoustic startle suppression
  - 2026 Behavioural Pharmacology: Corticosterone reduced, +282% center time (p=0.031) in chronic stress model; not replicated by standard CRF1 antagonist
  - Phase 1 ex-US (Dec 2025 topline): Completed SAD/MAD, well-tolerated, no SAEs
  - FDA pre-IND feedback Aug 20, 2026: No deficiencies
- **Files:** d1_pending/2026-11-17_inserts.sql, wp_pending/2026-11-17_pt-00114.json, forum_pending/20261117_pt-00114.php

**Why added:** PT-00114 is the first CRH-inhibiting peptide in the discovery pipeline and the first entry targeting the HPA axis stress pathway. The November 11 private placement announcement is the 7-day trigger. Completed Phase 1 ex-US data (December 2025) + FDA pre-IND clearance (August 2026) + 3 peer-reviewed preclinical publications satisfy the evidence criteria. Mechanism is genuinely distinct from all 22 existing site entries. HPA-axis modulation (CRF/cortisol reduction) is highly relevant to the fitness and longevity audience given cortisol's role in muscle catabolism, body composition, sleep quality, and neurological aging.
