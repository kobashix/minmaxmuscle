With both VANQUISH Phase 3 trials fully enrolled and oral Phase 2 results (12.2% weight loss at 13 weeks) supporting a planned oral Phase 3, VK2735 represents Viking's challenge to Lilly's tirzepatide dominance. The oral program is particularly compelling as it would position VK2735 against the oral semaglutide and oral ribupatide candidates also covered on the site.

**Berobenatide** (Pfizer, via Metsera acquisition) makes the case for once-monthly dosing as a commercial differentiator in the crowded GLP-1 market. The 12.3% placebo-adjusted weight loss at 28 weeks in VESPER-3 (monthly dosing Phase 2b) is the strongest published efficacy signal for a monthly injectable GLP-1 RA. With a 10-trial Phase 3 program and pivotal VESPER-6 enrolling, berobenatide is Pfizer's primary bet in obesity — and the first monthly dosing GLP-1 candidate in the site's coverage.
